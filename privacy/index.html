Privacy Policy

Agent Smith, operated by Jyls Treasures · Effective November 3, 2026

Agent Smith is a listing application used by Jyls Treasures to create and manage its eBay listings. This policy explains what information the application handles, why it handles it, how it is stored and protected, and the choices you have over it.

The short version

  • eBay data is used only for the eBay account that authorized the app, and only to create and manage that account’s listings.
  • Personal information is never sold, and eBay data is never used for advertising or shared with other sellers.
  • eBay credentials are held in server-side secrets and are never sent to your browser.
  • Listing photos are stored at public links, because eBay downloads listing images from a URL.

1. What this policy covers

This policy applies to the Agent Smith web application and its listing automation, including the pages used to run it, the records it stores about items, and the calls it makes to eBay on the seller’s behalf.

It does not cover eBay itself. eBay’s handling of your account, your orders and your buyers is governed by eBay’s User Privacy Notice and User Agreement, which you should read alongside this policy.

2. Who runs Agent Smith

Agent Smith is operated by Jyls Treasures, an independent eBay seller, for its own store and listings. It is not a data broker, it does not offer its services to other sellers, and it does not operate a shared pool of eBay data.

Jyls Treasures is the party responsible for the information described in this policy. Contact details are in section 17.

3. Information you create in the app

  • Account details: the email address, name and app role used to sign in.
  • Item photos: every photo uploaded for a batch of items.
  • Listing content: SKU, title, condition, item specifics, measurements, features, category, suggested price, item cost, and shipping and returns choices.
  • Settings: shipping policy profile IDs, origin ZIP code, fee and cost assumptions, daily publishing caps, publishing window and timezone.
  • Results recorded in the app: final sale prices and sale dates for items marked as sold.

4. Information read from your eBay account

The app can only reach eBay after you authorize it, and only for the one eBay account you authorize. Access is granted through eBay’s OAuth flow and can be revoked by you at any time.

With that authorization the app reads:

  • Your own listings and their eBay item identifiers, so the app can create, revise and renew them.
  • Your sold items and final sale prices, which the app uses as pricing comparisons for similar items.
  • Your seller standards profile and feedback score, for the seller health panel.
  • Your selling transactions, including shipping-label charges, which are used only to calculate an average shipping cost.

5. What the app does not do with eBay data

  • It does not sell, rent, license or publish eBay data.
  • It does not use eBay data for advertising, third-party profiling, or any purpose unrelated to these listings.
  • It does not share eBay data with other sellers or make it visible outside this account.
  • It does not scrape eBay or collect eBay content outside eBay’s official APIs.
  • It cannot read Seller Hub drafts: eBay does not provide an API for retrieving them, so drafts are only visible inside the eBay account.

6. How information is used

  • To analyze photos, group them into items and draft listing content.
  • To suggest a price from previous sales and comparable sold listings, and to keep it above the break-even floor.
  • To publish listings to eBay, and to renew or refresh listings that have gone stale.
  • To pace publishing against daily caps and the scheduled window.
  • To show dashboards covering items needing attention, pricing review, scheduled listings and seller health.
  • To diagnose errors and provide support when something goes wrong.

7. Automated processing and AI

Photos and item details are sent to AI models to identify what an item is and to draft its title, description and item specifics. Photo backgrounds are removed with image processing that works from the photo’s own pixels. Pricing may use a web search for comparable sold prices.

These outputs are proposals. Nothing is published to eBay until it is reviewed and confirmed, or until the seller’s own schedule publishes it. Only the content needed for the task is sent, and photos and listing content are not used to train models.

8. Legal bases for processing

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, processing relies on: performance of the service requested (managing listings), consent (connecting an eBay account and granting API access, which may be withdrawn at any time), legitimate interests (keeping the service secure, preventing abuse and fixing faults), and compliance with legal obligations such as tax and record-keeping.

9. When information is shared

  • With eBay, to create, revise, renew and monitor listings.
  • With service providers that host the application, store uploaded files, and run the AI models used to analyze photos and draft content. They may use the information only to provide those services.
  • With authorities or other parties when required by law, or to protect rights and safety.
  • With nobody else. Personal information is not sold, and eBay data is not shared with other sellers or advertisers.

10. Listing photos and public links

eBay fetches listing images from a URL, so uploaded photos are stored at public links that anyone holding the link can open, in the same form they appear on the listing. Images containing information that should not be public — documents, faces or addresses — should not be uploaded.

eBay credentials are treated differently: they are stored as server-side secrets, are never written into a public file, and are never sent to a browser.

11. How long information is kept

Listing records, photos and settings are kept while the account is in use, so listings can be reviewed, refreshed and renewed. Removing a batch or an item deletes that record from the app, and copies may remain in backups for a limited period before they expire.

eBay credentials are removed when access is revoked on eBay or the credentials are removed from the app’s settings.

12. How information is protected

  • All traffic between the browser, the app and eBay is encrypted in transit over HTTPS.
  • eBay credentials are stored as server-side secrets and are read only when a call to eBay is made.
  • Access to batches, items and settings requires signing in to the account.
  • Only the eBay permissions needed to run the listings are requested.
  • No system is completely secure. If a breach affects this information, affected parties and eBay will be notified as required by law.

13. Your choices and rights

You can ask to see the information held about you, have it corrected, exported or deleted. You can also object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Requests are handled within the time limits set by applicable law.

  • Revoke the app’s eBay access at any time from eBay’s third-party app access settings. The app then can no longer read or write listings.
  • Delete batches, items and photos from inside the app, or ask for them to be deleted.
  • Exercise these rights without being denied service or charged differently.
  • Personal information is not sold or shared for cross-context behavioural advertising, so no opt-out from sale is required.

14. Cookies and local storage

This website and the application use browser storage to keep users signed in and to remember a session. No advertising cookies, third-party tracking pixels or cross-site profiling are used.

15. Children

Agent Smith is a business tool for eBay sellers and is not intended for anyone under 18. eBay requires sellers to be at least 18 years old. Information from children is not knowingly collected; if you believe a child has provided information, please get in touch so it can be removed.

16. Where information is processed

The application and its service providers process information in the United States. Where information from other regions is processed there, transfers rely on the safeguards required by applicable law, such as standard contractual clauses.

17. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to access, correct, export or delete information, can be sent by eBay Messages to Jyls Treasures on eBay. Requests are answered within 30 days, or within the shorter period required by local law.

For questions about how eBay itself handles your data, contact eBay through eBay’s privacy centre.

18. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the application changes or as eBay’s requirements change. The current version is always published on this page with its effective date, and material changes are announced inside the app before they take effect.
